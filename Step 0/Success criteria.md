## Success criteria

The task is complete when:

* Coolify is installed and accessible over HTTPS.
* The Next.js app is deployed and accessible over HTTPS.
* The app deploys automatically when changes are merged into your feature branch.
* A small, focused content change has been submitted through a pull request.
* The pull request clearly explains the change, testing performed, and includes before and after screenshots.
* The merged change is visible on the live site.
* Deployment documentation explain how to deploy, make changes, and redeploy.
* No secrets, unrelated changes, or unnecessary dependencies have been added.

I will also evaluate:

* How independently you work.
* How clearly you communicate progress and blockers.
* How methodically you investigate problems.
* Whether you understand and verify AI-generated suggestions.
* The quality of your Git history and pull request.
* Whether the final setup is simple, reliable, and understandable.
